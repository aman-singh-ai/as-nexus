# Top AI Tools

Artificial Intelligence is changing the way we work. Here is a quick list of some amazing AI tools every developer and student should use:

1. **ChatGPT**: The best all-rounder for general knowledge, writing, and coding.
2. **Claude AI**: Excellent at parsing long documents and writing clean code.
3. **Perplexity**: The ultimate AI search engine that always cites its sources.
4. **Gemini**: Google's fast AI model deeply integrated with Google Workspace.
